What the statuses mean

Single answer / Written answer
A supported answer is supplied. Written questions do not need multiple-choice options.
Multiple valid choices
More than one offered choice satisfies the wording. Do not memorize an arbitrary single letter.
Interpretation needed
The conclusion changes with a missing assumption or a plausible reading. The answer explains those readings.
No correct listed option
The supported result or rule is supplied, but the source choices omit it or are malformed.
Missing question data
A necessary diagram, value or other premise is missing. Knowing the topic cannot recover unspecified data.
Additional reference needed
The unchanged complete note does not establish a fact needed to settle the item. This is an actual remaining limitation, not a broken link.
